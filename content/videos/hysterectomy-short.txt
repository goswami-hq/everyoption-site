Do I need a hysterectomy for fibroids? Hysterectomy removes your uterus, so fibroids don't come back. But many women don't need a hysterectomy. Uterine fibroid embolization (UFE) treats fibroids without surgery. Beads block their blood supply. Over the next few months, most fibroids shrink. Symptoms improve for most women.
