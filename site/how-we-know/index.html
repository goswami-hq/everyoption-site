How we know

Every doctor on a procedure page is there because of something we can show you. The evidence sits under their name, with a link to where it came from.

What the two groups mean

Performs this procedure
The doctor’s own page says they perform it (for example, “she performs uterine fibroid embolization” or “has extensive experience in” it).
Lists this procedure; confirm with the practice
A page lists or mentions the procedure, but does not say this doctor performs it: a list of services, a practice page, a search-result preview, a directory listing, a ClinicalTrials.gov study of the procedure they lead, or public Medicare billing for an embolization code shared by several procedures together with a page that names this one. Call the practice before you book.

We only show a doctor when we have one of these. People with weaker evidence are not shown.

Where the evidence comes from

  • Doctors’ own pages: their profile on a hospital, university or practice website.
  • Practice pages: the services page of the practice they work in.
  • Public Medicare billing records: the yearly files Medicare publishes on what each doctor billed. We show the code and the year.
  • ClinicalTrials.gov: the U.S. registry of clinical studies, where study leaders are named.
  • Search-engine results: the short preview a search engine shows of a page we could not read in full. The card says when evidence comes from one.
  • Directory listings: other websites that copy a doctor’s profile. The card says when evidence comes from one.

Cities come from the doctor’s newest page. When we only have a city from public records, the card says “City from public records; confirm location with the practice.” Distances are measured between ZIP code centres, not street addresses.

A blank is not a no

If a doctor is missing, or shows only in the second group, it usually means we have not found a page that says more. It does not mean they don’t perform the procedure.

The badge under a name

  • Interventional radiologist: their own page states interventional radiology training or board certification.
  • Does IR procedures: their own page says they perform catheter procedures that interventional radiologists do.
  • Bills IR procedures: public Medicare billing records show interventional radiology procedures.

Who runs this

Every Option is run by a practicing interventional radiologist. Listings are free, and no one can pay to be listed, ranked higher, or sent patients.

Within each group, the order is by distance only.

Report an error

If something is wrong, or you are a listed doctor and want to correct or claim your listing, use the correction form or email errors@everyoptionmd.com. A person reviews every report before anything changes.
