Find a doctor for prostate artery embolization (PAE)

Prostate artery embolization (PAE) is for an enlarged prostate that causes urinary problems, like going often, getting up at night or a weak stream. It’s a minimally invasive procedure: a doctor works through a small puncture in the wrist or groin and cuts down the blood going to the prostate, so it shrinks over time. Talk to a doctor about whether it’s right for you.
