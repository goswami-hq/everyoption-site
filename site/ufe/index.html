Find a doctor for uterine fibroid embolization (UFE)

Uterine fibroid embolization (UFE) treats fibroids that cause heavy bleeding, pain or pressure. A doctor blocks the fibroids’ blood supply through a small puncture in an artery, and the uterus stays in place.

In a Cochrane review of randomized trials, satisfaction was similar to surgery and women returned to normal activities about two weeks sooner, but more needed another procedure within two years (moderate-quality evidence).

It is not for pregnancy, an active infection of the uterus, or uterine or ovarian cancer (CIRSE standards of practice, 2015). If you plan a pregnancy, guidelines differ; ask about myomectomy too.

Sources: Cochrane systematic review of embolization versus surgery for fibroids (6 randomized trials, 640 women at 24 months); CIRSE standards of practice (van Overhagen and Reekers, 2015); ACR Appropriateness Criteria, Management of Uterine Fibroids (2023).

This list is not a recommendation or a ranking. Whether UFE is right for you is a decision for you and your doctor. How we know.

Education, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your own care. In an emergency, call 911.
