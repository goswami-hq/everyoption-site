Find a doctor for uterine fibroid embolization (UFE)

Uterine fibroid embolization (UFE) is for fibroids that cause heavy periods, pain, or pressure. It’s a minimally invasive procedure: a doctor works through a small puncture in the wrist or groin and cuts down the blood going to the fibroids, so they shrink over time. You keep your uterus. Talk to a doctor about whether it’s right for you.
