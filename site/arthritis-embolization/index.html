About knee artery embolization (GAE)

Genicular artery embolization (GAE) is a newer treatment for knee arthritis pain. Tiny particles slow extra blood flow to the lining of the knee to calm swelling and pain. You keep your own knee.12

How it works

It is done through a tiny skin nick, usually in the groin. You stay awake, with medicine to help you relax. Most people go home the same day. Relief often starts within about two weeks.12

How well does it work?

Early results are encouraging. Many people feel better, and for some the relief lasts at least two years. Doctors are still learning how much of this relief comes from the treatment itself. Larger trials are under way. If GAE works for you, it may help you put off a knee replacement, but we don't know that yet.
The Society of Interventional Radiology says GAE should be offered to people whose knee pain has not improved with other treatments, or who cannot have, do not want, or want to delay a knee replacement.345678

Side effects

You may have a bruise where the tube went in. About 1 to 3 out of 10 people get a patchy color change or redness on the skin over the knee. It usually fades within a month. Some people get a small skin sore over the knee. It usually heals on its own. Rarely, numbness or tingling in the lower leg can last for months. Serious problems are rare.14931011

Results vary from person to person. Talk to a doctor about whether this is right for you.

Embolization for other joints

Doctors are also trying embolization for arthritis in other joints. For these joints, it is too early to know how well it works.121314

Hip. Early studies suggest it may ease hip pain for up to a year.1213

Hand and fingers. It is not yet well studied.14

Shoulder. There are no studies yet of embolization for shoulder arthritis.15

Frozen shoulder is not arthritis. It is a stiff, painful shoulder from a swollen, scarred joint lining. It often gets better on its own, but this can take up to 3 years. Early studies suggest embolization may ease night pain within weeks. Rare serious problems, such as brief loss of vision, have been reported.161718

The doctor finder on this page lists doctors who perform knee embolization. It does not show who treats other joints.

Osteoarthritis is the most common joint problem. It happens when the cushion in a joint wears down over time. It cannot be cured, but the pain can often be eased. If knee pain limits your life, embolization is not your only option. You can also consider exercise, medicines, injections, or surgery. The right choice depends on your symptoms and goals.19

If a joint suddenly turns hot, red, and swollen, you cannot put weight on it, you have a fever, or you feel very sick or faint, call 911.

Your other options for knee arthritis

Exercise and medicines

These are the first steps for most people. No procedure is needed.

Exercise and weight loss

Exercise and physical therapy build strength and keep the knee moving. Losing weight, if you are overweight, takes stress off the knee.19

How invasive is it?
No procedure is needed. A physical therapist can show you what to do.19
How well does it work?
Good evidence suggests exercise helps you use your knee better. It may also ease pain a little. If you are overweight, losing weight with diet and exercise can ease pain more than exercise alone. If physical therapy has not helped after 6 to 12 weeks, it likely will not.202119
Side effects
Pain can make it hard to keep active. Ask your doctor for an exercise routine.19
Pain relievers

These include acetaminophen and anti-inflammatory pills like ibuprofen and naproxen. Creams that go on the skin are another option.19

How invasive is it?
These are pills or creams. No procedure is needed.
How well does it work?
Strong evidence shows anti-inflammatory pills ease knee pain. Anti-inflammatory gels on the skin also help some people, with fewer side effects. Acetaminophen helps very little. None of these fix the knee.222319
Side effects
Do not take more than 4,000 mg of acetaminophen a day. Anti-inflammatory pills can raise the risk of heart attack, stroke, and stomach bleeding. Talk with your doctor before taking them often.1924
Other medicines

Duloxetine is a prescription pill that can help long-term arthritis pain. Opioid pain pills, including tramadol, are another kind of prescription pill.19

How invasive is it?
These are pills. No procedure is needed.
How well does it work?
Good evidence suggests duloxetine eases long-term knee pain a little. Opioid pills ease arthritis pain only slightly.252627
Side effects
Side effects make some people stop duloxetine. Opioid pills cause more side effects than they are worth for most people with arthritis.252226
Less invasive treatments

These are done without surgery. You keep your own knee. Before you agree to surgery, ask about these.

Knee artery embolization (GAE)Details above
Steroid shot

A steroid medicine is injected into the knee.19

How invasive is it?
It is a shot given during an office visit.
How well does it work?
Studies disagree on how much a steroid shot helps. It often eases pain for a while, but the relief usually fades by about 3 months. Getting shots again and again does not seem to give more relief. Physical therapy may help more than shots over a year.19282930
Side effects
Repeat shots may wear down the knee cushion a little faster.29
Gel shots (hyaluronic acid)

A gel is injected into the knee. It aims to replace the joint fluid that thins with arthritis.32

How invasive is it?
They are shots given during office visits.
How well does it work?
Gel shots ease pain only slightly more than fake shots, too little for most people to notice.31
Side effects
Serious problems are a bit more common than with fake shots. The knee can be sore or swollen for a short time.3132
Platelet-rich plasma (PRP)

Platelets from your own blood are injected into the knee.32

How invasive is it?
Blood is drawn and the shots are given during office visits.33
How well does it work?
Studies disagree on whether PRP helps. It may ease pain for up to 6 months. By one year, it may do no better than a salt water shot.3433
Side effects
Side effects are usually mild and short-lived. The knee can be sore or swollen for a short time.33
Nerve ablation

Heat quiets the nerves that carry pain from the knee.32

How invasive is it?
Ask your doctor how it is done and how long recovery takes.
How well does it work?
Studies disagree on whether it helps. It may ease pain for about 3 months. Longer relief has not been shown.3536
Side effects
Serious problems seem no more common than with a fake procedure.35
Surgery

Surgery replaces the worn parts of the knee or changes how weight rests on it.

Knee replacement

Surgery removes the worn cartilage and bone. Metal and plastic parts replace them.37

How invasive is it?
Surgery takes 1 to 2 hours. Most people leave the hospital in 1 to 4 days. Most daily activities return in 3 to 6 weeks. Full recovery can take several months.3738
How well does it work?
Strong evidence shows most new knees last a long time. About 8 out of 10 still have their original parts 25 years later. For moderate to severe arthritis, a knee replacement can ease pain more than non-surgery care. About 1 to 2 out of 10 people are not satisfied with their new knee.39404142
Side effects
Serious problems, like a knee infection, happen in fewer than 2 out of 100 people. Serious problems are more likely with surgery than without it. Some people have ongoing pain or stiffness.374043
Partial knee replacement

Surgery replaces only the worn part of the knee.19

How invasive is it?
Recovery should be shorter than after a full replacement. You may drive again after about 3 weeks instead of 6.38
How well does it work?
It is an option when only one part of the knee is worn. Five years later, results are about the same as with a full replacement. A partial knee is more likely to need redoing later on.4439
Side effects
Infection, blood clots, and ongoing pain or stiffness can happen after knee replacement.43
Bone realignment (osteotomy)

Surgery reshapes the shin bone to shift weight off the worn inner side of the knee. It is mainly for younger, active people with bow legs.4645

How invasive is it?
It is surgery on the bone near the knee.19
How well does it work?
Early studies suggest it eases symptoms more than non-surgery care. About 1 out of 10 people have a knee replacement more than 10 years later.4546
Side effects
Ask your surgeon about its risks and how long recovery takes.
Knee scope clean-out

A camera and small tools wash out or trim the inside of the knee.1947

How invasive is it?
It is surgery done through the skin with a camera.19
How well does it work?
Strong evidence shows a knee scope clean-out does not help knee arthritis. A major guideline advises against it for knee arthritis.474849
Side effects
It carries the risks of surgery, such as blood clots and infection.50

What is arthritis of the knee?

In arthritis, the cushion between the bones wears away. The bones can rub together. This causes pain, swelling, and stiffness. Arthritis is the most common disease of the knee.1951

Pain is often worse when you walk or put weight on the knee. Stiffness after waking often lasts 30 minutes or less. Later, pain can come even at rest or wake you at night. See a doctor if your symptoms bother you or get worse.19

What should I ask my doctor?
  • Which options could work for me, and which could not?
  • What has my knee X-ray shown?
  • How long would I need to recover?
  • Do you offer knee embolization, or can you refer me to someone who does?
Common questions

Do I need treatment?

Not always. Many people manage with exercise, weight loss if needed, and pain relievers. These treatments cannot make arthritis go away, but they can often delay surgery.19

Does embolization fix the arthritis?

No. It aims to ease pain. The worn cushion in the joint is still there. Scans have not shown that it slows the arthritis.252

Can I still have a knee replacement later?

Yes. GAE does not seem to make a later knee replacement harder or less successful, but doctors are still checking.4

Do I need a knee replacement?

Not always. It is usually for severe arthritis when other treatments no longer help. Many people try exercise, medicines, or injections first. Some also consider embolization. Many people who could have a knee replacement try a full non-surgery program first and still have not had surgery 2 years later. If other treatments have stopped helping, waiting longer for surgery is usually not advised.19375354
