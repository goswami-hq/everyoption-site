Find a doctor for genicular artery embolization (GAE)

Genicular artery embolization (GAE) is for knee pain from arthritis that hasn’t gotten better with other treatments. It’s a minimally invasive procedure: a doctor works through a small puncture in an artery and cuts down the extra blood flow that feeds inflammation in the knee, which can ease the pain. It’s a newer option. Talk to a doctor about whether it’s right for you.
