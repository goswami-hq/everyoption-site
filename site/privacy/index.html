Every Option privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Last updated: November 13, 2026

East Blue Holdings LLC ("Every Option", "we", "us") runs everyoptionmd.com and the Every Option service. This policy describes the information we handle and why. It covers people reading the site, people who send us a report or a message, and physicians who make an account. Every Option is an educational publication. It is not a health care provider, and it does not collect medical records.

1. Reading the site and using the finder

1.1 You do not need an account to read pages, watch videos or search for a doctor.

1.2 The full ZIP code you type into the doctor finder is used in your browser. To show nearby doctors, your browser downloads directory data for a wide area, named by the first three digits of your ZIP, and for the procedure you are searching.

1.3 Like every website, our hosting and security provider processes your device's internet address and basic technical details (such as browser type and the page requested) to deliver pages and protect the service, and it may set a cookie for security. We use this information to run and protect the service.

1.4 We, and companies that work with us, may use cookies, pixels and similar tools to understand how the service is used, to measure and show advertising, and to protect the service. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

1.5 Videos and links may take you to other platforms (such as YouTube, Instagram or TikTok). Those platforms have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

2. Reports and messages

2.1 If you report an error or write to us, we receive what you send, including your email if you give it, and use it to review the listing, to reply, and to run and protect the service.

2.2 Do not send medical details about yourself or anyone else.

2.3 To limit repeated or abusive use, we process internet addresses in scrambled form for a short time.

3. Physicians with an account

3.1 What we collect. Your NPI and the name and specialty the NPI Registry holds for it; your login email; what you tell us about your specialty and the procedures you perform, with the words you agreed to and the date; what you add to your listing (for example notes, insurance plans, offices, phone numbers, links, a photo); invitations you send or accept; and a record of changes to your account and listing.

3.2 Why. To check that you are the physician, sign you in, show your listing, contact you about your account or the service, keep a record of what was shown and when, and run, protect and improve the service.

3.3 From others. We use public sources, such as the NPI Registry and practice websites, to build listings and to find a published email address for an invitation. If you choose to confirm your identity through an outside provider or a professional network, we receive the result and details needed to match you to your NPI record. That provider's own privacy policy covers what you give it.

3.4 What is public. Your name, the procedures on your listing, and what you add to your listing are public. They may be copied or shown by search engines, AI assistants and others, and we cannot control or recall those copies.

3.5 Office addresses. We may send an office address to a mapping service to get its map coordinates.

4. Who we share it with

4.1 Service providers that help us run the service, such as hosting, security, database, email, mapping, analytics, advertising and identity-check providers.

4.2 Others when we believe it is needed to follow the law or a legal request, to protect people, our rights or the service, or as part of a sale, merger or transfer of Every Option or its assets.

4.3 We do not sell personal information for money.

5. How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, including keeping records of listings and agreements, resolving disputes and meeting legal duties. When you close your account, what you added is removed from public view on our site.

6. Your choices

6.1 Physicians can change or remove what they added in their account.

6.2 You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to accounts@everyoptionmd.com. We will respond as the law that applies to you requires. We may keep information where the law allows, for example records of agreements.

6.3 A physician may ask us to correct or remove a listing through the report form or the address above.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee security.

8. Children

The service is meant for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy by posting a new version with a new date.

10. Contact

East Blue Holdings LLC, 8735 Dunwoody Pl, Ste N, Atlanta, GA 30350, accounts@everyoptionmd.com.

Education, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your own care. In an emergency, call 911.

Found an error? Report an error or email errors@everyoptionmd.com.

Every Option is run by a practicing interventional radiologist. Listings are free, and no one can pay to be listed, ranked higher, or sent patients.
