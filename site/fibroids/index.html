Shrink fibroids without surgery

Fibroid embolization works through a tiny skin nick. Your uterus stays in place. Most people go home the same day.
Recovery takes about 1 week instead of 2 to 6 weeks after major surgery. How it works

Fibroid embolization (UFE)

Tiny beads block blood flow to the fibroids so they shrink. You keep your uterus.1132

How invasive is it?
You only need a tiny skin nick. There is no cut in the belly, so no belly scar where a hernia can form. Most women go home the same day. Recovery takes about 1 week, instead of 2 to 6 weeks after major surgery.1234
How well does it work?
Most women feel much better. Almost 9 out of 10 women see their symptoms improve. About 7 out of 10 women still feel better five years later. Most women feel just as satisfied as those who had surgery.
Some women may need more treatment: about 1 out of 4 may need another procedure down the line.
After ten years, about 2 out of 3 women avoided a hysterectomy entirely.5678910
Side effects
You may have cramps, nausea, and a low fever for up to a week. Less than 5 out of 100 women go through menopause early. Serious problems are no more common than after surgery. Smaller problems are more common.111712

Results vary from person to person. Talk to a doctor about whether this is right for you.

Find a doctor for fibroid embolization

Fibroids are growths in the uterus. They are not cancer. Many women have no symptoms and need no treatment. If you have heavy periods, pain, or pressure, embolization is not your only option. You can also consider medicines, other less invasive treatments, or surgery. The right choice depends on your symptoms and goals.13

If you have severe bleeding, severe pain, a fever, or feel very sick or faint, call 911.

Your other options

Medicines

These treat symptoms like heavy bleeding and pain. No procedure is needed.

Medicines without hormones

These include tranexamic acid, which you take only on period days, plus pain relievers and iron.131415

How invasive is it?
These are pills. No procedure is needed.
How well does it work?
They ease bleeding and cramps. They do not treat the fibroids, so symptoms return when you stop.1314
Side effects
Tranexamic acid can rarely cause blood clots.14
Hormone medicines

These include birth control pills or shots, daily pills that lower hormones, and short-term shots before surgery.1319

How invasive is it?
These are pills or shots. No procedure is needed.
How well does it work?
Daily hormone-lowering pills greatly reduced bleeding for about 7 out of 10 women. Symptoms often return after stopping.1615
Side effects
You may have hot flashes. Some of these medicines weaken bones, so use is limited. They are not for women who have had blood clots.171819
Hormone IUD

A small device placed in the uterus thins the lining. It lasts up to 5 years.1521

How invasive is it?
It is placed during an office visit.
How well does it work?
It lightens periods. It does not treat the fibroids.15
Side effects
You may have spotting at first. It is more likely to slip out when fibroids are present.20
Less invasive treatments

These treat fibroids or bleeding without major surgery. Your uterus stays in place. Before you agree to surgery, ask about these.

Fibroid embolization (UFE)Details above
Radiofrequency ablation

Heat from a small needle destroys the fibroid.13

How invasive is it?
The doctor uses small cuts in the belly, or goes through the vagina with no cuts. Most women go home the same day.2223
How well does it work?
Results vary from study to study. About 1 out of 11 women needed surgery within 3 years in one study, and about 2 out of 5 within 2 years in another.2224
Side effects
You may have pain and cramps. Serious problems were rare in studies.2224
Focused ultrasound

Sound waves, guided by an MRI scanner, heat and shrink the fibroid.11

How invasive is it?
There is no cut. Most women recover quickly.11
How well does it work?
It fits only some fibroids. Some women need more treatment later. Not all insurance covers it.112526
Side effects
You may have belly pain. Skin burns or bruises can happen and usually heal in a week or two.11
Endometrial ablation

Heat or cold destroys the lining of the uterus to lighten periods.27

How invasive is it?
There are no cuts. Most women go home the same day and recover in 1 to 2 days.27
How well does it work?
It treats the lining, not the fibroids, so they stay in place. A radiology group rates it usually not appropriate for fibroids. In studies of heavy bleeding, about 1 out of 8 women had a hysterectomy within 5 years.132829
Side effects
You may have cramps and discharge for a while. It is not for anyone who may want a future pregnancy.2730
Surgery

Surgery removes the fibroids or the whole uterus.

Removal through the cervix

A thin camera and small tools remove fibroids growing inside the uterus.1331

How invasive is it?
There are no cuts. Recovery takes a few days.3
How well does it work?
It only works for fibroids inside the uterus. New fibroids can form later.1315
Side effects
You may have cramps and light bleeding for a few days. Heavy bleeding, infection, or a tear in the uterus are rare.31
Myomectomy

Surgery removes the fibroids and leaves the uterus.13

How invasive is it?
It takes a larger cut in the belly or a few small cuts. Recovery takes about 2 to 6 weeks.3
How well does it work?
It removes the fibroids you have, but new ones can grow. Women's quality of life improves. About 1 out of 8 women may need another procedure down the line.133329
Side effects
Bleeding, infection, blood clots, and harm to nearby organs can happen but are not common. Scar tissue forms inside the belly in most women. It can make organs stick together. It often causes no symptoms, but it can cause pain or block the bowel. After open surgery, up to 1 out of 5 people get a hernia at the scar.3333435
Hysterectomy

Surgery removes the whole uterus.4

How invasive is it?
The doctor goes through the vagina, small cuts, or a larger cut in the belly. Recovery takes about 2 to 6 weeks.4
How well does it work?
It is the only treatment that keeps fibroids from returning. Periods stop.153
Side effects
Bleeding, infection, blood clots, and harm to nearby organs can happen. Menopause begins if the ovaries are also removed. Scar tissue can make organs stick together and, rarely, block the bowel. After open surgery, up to 1 out of 5 people get a hernia at the scar.4333435

What are fibroids?

Fibroids are muscle growths in or on the wall of the uterus. They are very common. By age 50, about 2 to 8 out of 10 women have them.15

Symptoms can include heavy or long periods, tiredness from blood loss, pelvic pain or pressure, frequent urination, and pain during sex. See a doctor if you have any of these, or if you soak through a pad or tampon every hour for 2 to 3 hours.131536

What should I ask my doctor?
  • Which options could work for me, and which could not?
  • How would each option affect a future pregnancy?
  • How long would I need to recover?
  • Do you offer embolization, or can you refer me to someone who does?
Common questions

Do I need treatment?

Not always. If you have no symptoms or only mild ones, your doctor can watch the fibroids with exams or an ultrasound. They often shrink after menopause.1315

Are fibroids cancer?

No. Rarely, a growth that looks like a fibroid turns out to be cancer. This happens in less than 1 out of 200 women who have fibroid surgery.1537

Can I get pregnant after treatment?

It depends on the treatment and your situation. If you hope to get pregnant, ask your doctor which options fit you before you choose. Pregnancy is not possible after a hysterectomy and can be dangerous after endometrial ablation.330

Do I need a hysterectomy for fibroids?

Not always. Many women never need one. It is the only treatment that keeps fibroids from returning. Medicines, less invasive treatments, and surgery that removes only the fibroids are also options.1315
