About prostate artery embolization (PAE)

Tiny beads slow blood flow to the prostate so it shrinks. Urine can then flow more easily.12

How it works

You only need a tiny skin nick in the wrist or groin. You stay awake, with medicine to help you relax. Nothing is cut out. Most men do not need a urine tube through the penis. Most men go home the same day. Most men are back to normal activities within a few days. Avoid heavy activity for about a week. After TURP surgery, recovery takes 3 to 6 weeks.123456

How well does it work?

Most men feel much better. About 8 out of 10 men still felt better up to three years later. After a year, men who had PAE may feel their symptoms improve by about the same amount as men who had TURP surgery.
Some men may need more treatment: about 1 out of 5 had another procedure within five years, such as a second embolization or surgery. Results after surgery are more likely to last.7891011

Side effects

You may have a burning feeling in your lower belly, need to pee often, and some pelvic pain for a few days. A little blood in your urine or stool can happen. Erections usually stay the same. Some men may have less semen afterward, but it is much less common than after TURP surgery. Serious problems are rare.1129138

Results vary from person to person. Talk to a doctor about whether this is right for you.

Find a doctor for prostate artery embolization

An enlarged prostate is common as men age. This is not cancer and does not raise your risk of prostate cancer. Many men have no symptoms. If you have trouble peeing, embolization is one of many options. You can also consider medicines, other less invasive treatments, or surgery. The right choice depends on your symptoms and goals.1415

If you cannot pee at all, have a fever with chills, see a lot of blood or clots in your urine, have severe pain, or feel very sick or faint, call 911.

Your other options

Medicines

These ease symptoms like frequent peeing and a weak stream. No procedure is needed.

Alpha blockers

These include pills (such as tamsulosin). They relax the muscles around the prostate and bladder so urine flows more easily.1415

How invasive is it?
These are pills. No procedure is needed.
How well does it work?
Most men notice their symptoms improve within 3 to 7 days.14
Side effects
You may feel dizzy, mostly when you stand up. Ejaculation may change. If you plan eye surgery for cataracts, tell your eye doctor if you take one or have taken one.16
Prostate-shrinking pills

These are pills (such as finasteride or dutasteride). They lower a hormone that makes the prostate grow, so it slowly shrinks.141517

How invasive is it?
These are pills. No procedure is needed.
How well does it work?
It can take 3 to 6 months to feel better. They lower the chance of needing surgery. In a large trial, finasteride cut the chance of needing surgery by about half over 4 years. Taking one with an alpha blocker may work better than either alone.1417181915
Side effects
You may have a lower sex drive or erection problems. They cut your PSA level about in half, so tell your doctor before a PSA test. They may raise the chance of a more serious prostate cancer.141718
Tadalafil

This is a pill. It relaxes muscles around the bladder and prostate. It is also used for erection problems.1520

How invasive is it?
This is a pill. No procedure is needed.
How well does it work?
It can ease some symptoms.1520
Side effects
You may have a headache, upset stomach, or back pain. Do not take it with nitrate heart medicines. It is not recommended with alpha blockers for this use.20
Less invasive treatments

These treat the prostate without major surgery. Nothing is cut out of your body. Before you agree to surgery, ask about these.

Prostate artery embolization (PAE)Details above
Urethral lift

Small implants lift and hold the prostate away from the urethra, like tiebacks on a curtain.1521

How invasive is it?
It is done through the penis, with no cuts in the skin. Most men need little or no urine tube time and return to normal activity in about 1 week.2122
How well does it work?
It was tested only in some prostate sizes. Symptoms improve within 2 weeks. About 1 out of 7 men needed another procedure within 5 years.22
Side effects
Side effects were mostly mild and short-lived. Erections and ejaculation stayed the same in the trial.22
Water vapor (steam) therapy

Steam is released into the prostate to burn the inside of the prostate. This removes some of the extra tissue and makes the path for urine larger.1521

How invasive is it?
It is done through the penis, with no cuts in the skin. It can be done in the doctor's office.21
How well does it work?
It was tested only in some prostate sizes. Symptoms improve within 3 months. About 1 out of 25 men needed surgery within 5 years.23
Side effects
Side effects were mostly mild and went away quickly. No lasting erection problems were reported in the trial.2423
Temporary implant

A small folded device is placed in the prostate for about a week. As it opens, it makes small cuts that widen the passage. Then it is removed.2125

How invasive is it?
It is placed and later removed through the penis, with no cuts in the skin.2125
How well does it work?
Symptoms improved for about 8 out of 10 men at 3 months. But about 6 out of 10 men improved after a fake procedure, too. Longer results come from a smaller study.2526
Side effects
Side effects were mostly mild and short-lived. No new erection or ejaculation problems were seen in the trial.25
Medicine-coated balloon

A balloon passed through the penis widens the space where the prostate squeezes the urethra. The balloon is coated with a medicine that helps keep it from closing again.29

How invasive is it?
It is done through the penis, with no cuts in the skin. It can be done in the office.27
How well does it work?
It was tested only in some prostate sizes. About 7 out of 10 men had a good response at 2 years.2728
Side effects
You may have blood in your urine or a bladder infection. Sexual function stayed the same in the trial.28
Surgery

Surgery removes the part of the prostate that blocks the flow of urine.

Transurethral resection (TURP)

Surgery removes the inside of the prostate with a small tool passed through the penis.30

How invasive is it?
There are no cuts in the skin. You are asleep or numb from the waist down. Most men stay in the hospital for 1 to 3 days and have a urine tube for 1 to 3 days. Recovery takes about 3 to 6 weeks.306
How well does it work?
It relieves symptoms most of the time. About 1 out of 10 men need more surgery within 20 years.3015
Side effects
Many men have dry orgasms afterward, where semen goes into the bladder. This can be permanent. Bleeding, infection, erection problems, and leaking urine can happen. Scar tissue can narrow the urethra.631301532
Laser surgery

A laser passed through the penis removes or vaporizes the blocking tissue. One type is called HoLEP.21

How invasive is it?
There are no cuts in the skin. Most men go home the same day or the next day, with a urine tube for a few days. Compared with TURP, men had less bleeding and shorter hospital stays.2133
How well does it work?
It improves the flow of urine. HoLEP removed more tissue than TURP in trials.2133
Side effects
Many men have dry orgasms afterward. Erection problems and leaking urine can happen.3121
Waterjet surgery

High-pressure jets of water, passed through the penis, remove the blocking tissue.21

How invasive is it?
There are no cuts in the skin. You have a urine tube for a few days.21
How well does it work?
In a trial, it worked as well as TURP for 5 years, for prostates of small to medium size.34
Side effects
Fewer men had ejaculation problems than after TURP, but some still did: about 1 out of 14. Erection problems and leaking urine can happen.3421
Open or robotic prostate removal

Surgery through the belly removes the inside of a very large prostate.35

How invasive is it?
It takes a cut in the lower belly, or small cuts with a robot. After open surgery, most men stay in the hospital for 2 to 4 days with a urine tube. Recovery takes about 6 weeks.3515
How well does it work?
It is often used when the prostate is too large for other surgery. Most men can pee as usual afterward.35
Side effects
Dry orgasms, leaking urine, bleeding, and infection can happen. After open surgery, up to 1 out of 5 people get a hernia at the scar.3536

What is an enlarged prostate?

The prostate is a small gland that wraps around the urethra, the tube that carries urine out of the body. In almost all men, it grows with age. It can squeeze the urethra and make it hard to empty the bladder. As men get older, this gets more common, with more than 9 out of 10 men having it in their 80s.1415

Symptoms can include frequent peeing, a strong and sudden urge to go, getting up at night to pee, a weak or slow stream, straining, and dribbling at the end. See a doctor if your bladder does not feel empty after you pee, or if self-care has not helped after 2 months. A doctor also checks for other causes, such as infection or cancer.1415

What should I ask my doctor?
  • Which options could work for me, and which could not?
  • How would each option affect my erections and ejaculation?
  • How long would I need to recover, and will I need a urine tube?
  • Do you offer prostate artery embolization, or can you refer me to someone who does?
Common questions

Do I need treatment?

Not always. If your symptoms are mild and do not bother you, your doctor may watch them with yearly checkups. Drinking less before bed and cutting back on alcohol and caffeine can help.15

Is an enlarged prostate cancer?

No. It is not cancer, and it does not raise your risk of prostate cancer. Some symptoms can look alike, so your doctor may check your PSA, a blood test, to screen you for prostate cancer.1415

Will treatment affect my sex life?

It depends on the treatment. Many surgeries cause dry orgasms, where semen goes into the bladder. Some pills can lower your sex drive or cause erection problems. Ask your doctor how each option fits your goals before you choose.3117

Do I need surgery for an enlarged prostate?

Not always. Medicines and less invasive treatments, such as PAE (prostate artery embolization), are also options. Surgery is often used when other treatments have not helped or problems develop.15
